# GigiClick

**Hold left click to auto click at a random rate.**

Tired of spam clicking? Hold left click and GigiClick does it for you, at a random speed inside whatever range you set. Let go and it stops.

## Features
- **Hold to click.** It only clicks while you're holding the button.
- **Random CPS range.** Every click lands at a different speed between your min and max.
- **Settings menu.** Drag the slider or pick a preset (Relaxed, Steady, Fast, Turbo). There's a little live preview too.
- **HUD.** Shows your CPS in the corner while you click. Can be turned off.
- **Stays out of the way.** Pauses in menus and chat, and mining still works like normal.
- **Client-side only.** Nothing needed on the server.

## Controls
| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Hold left click | Auto click |
| `G` | Turn on/off |
| `Right Shift` or `/gigiclick` | Open settings |
| `/gigiclick cps <min> <max>` | Set your range |

All keys can be rebound in **Controls → GigiClick**.

## Requirements
- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) (required)
- [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) (optional, adds a Configure button)

## Multiplayer
**Don't use this on servers.** Most of them ban auto clickers. It's made for singleplayer.
